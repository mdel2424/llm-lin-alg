Scope & sources

About this course

For someone returning to linear algebra after a first university course. Start at the beginning or use the chapters as a reference.

Small models, real computation

Every plot and readout is calculated from the controls. The geometry uses two dimensions; attention uses three tokens and two features. Embeddings and weights are chosen for teaching, not learned from text. Token labels do not demonstrate semantic understanding.

Numerical experiments build intuition. The proof workshops give separate deductive arguments and state their assumptions. This course prepares you for elementary rank, norm and attention arguments; it is not a complete treatment of optimization, probability or LLM theory.
